## What this is
The FIRST and ONLY ai_query()/LLM call in the build, and it is ADVISORY. The flag
AUTHORITY remains the deterministic UC function check_toxicity_flag (stage 02). Here
the LLM turns already-computed deterministic facts (the flag reason text + the clinical
correlation state) into a natural-language recommended NEXT ACTION. It invents no data.
Every recommendation row logs its exact input prompt, the model endpoint, and a
generated_ts, so the audit trail now covers AI-generated GUIDANCE as well as the human
resolution decisions already logged in Lakebase (stage 03).
