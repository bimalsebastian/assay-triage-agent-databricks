## Policy definitions (bodies committed in transforms/19_clinical_rls_masks.sql)
mask_clinical_value(v)  -> CASE WHEN is_account_group_member('clinical_pis') THEN v
                                ELSE 'REDACTED — clinical detail (clinical_pis only)' END
mask_patient_key(p)     -> CASE WHEN is_account_group_member('clinical_pis') THEN p
                                ELSE concat('anon-', substr(sha2(p,256),1,10)) END
rf_clinical_obs(obs)    -> is_account_group_member('clinical_pis') OR obs <> 'lab_result'

## Why this matters
The tiered clinical-governance story ("chemists see aggregates, PIs see masked detail") is
now enforced by Unity Catalog itself as a visible artifact — not just described in grants
or comments. A clinical_pis member sees full detail; every other identity is masked and
row-filtered, per-person, at query time.
